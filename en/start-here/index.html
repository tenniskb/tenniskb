Start Here

New to the system? Start with these 3 simple steps.

Step 1: Understand the CORE Map

The entire biomechanical system is built on 12 interconnected variables forming a single chain:

Q → V → ROOT → F → P → T → SSC → 8 → 45° → BRAKE → PULSE → JIN

Quiet Eye → Vestibular → Rooting → Fascia → Proprioception → Tensegrity → SSC (Stretch-Shortening Cycle) → Figure-8 → 45° Swing Plane → Brake → Pulse / Impulse → Jin

Step 2: Find Your Broken Link

Every stroke error traces back to one broken variable. Diagnosing it takes 5 minutes.

Your SymptomLikely Broken Link
Late contact, off-center hitsQ (Quiet Eye)
Head bobbing, losing balance while movingV (Vestibular)
No power, body falling backwardROOT (Rooting)
Arming the ball, elbow/shoulder painF/P (Fascia / Proprioception)
Stiff muscle force, no whipT/SSC (Tensegrity / Stretch-Shortening)
Racquet opens too wide, late contact8 (Figure-8 regulation)
Flat shots, net errors or long balls45° (Swing Plane)
Weak shots, no penetrationBRAKE / PULSE (Braking & Impulse)
Inconsistent under match pressureJIN (Unified Power)

Step 3: Follow Your Path

Three Golden Principles

  1. No variable works alone. The chain is only as strong as its weakest link.
  2. Don't skip links. You cannot fix PULSE if your ROOT is broken.
  3. Train the whole chain, not in isolation. Every drill connects to the unified system.
